Livestock Care

Record concerns about cattle, goats, sheep, poultry, pigs and other animals. ShambaSense can help organise observations, but it is not a veterinary service and cannot confirm animal disease.

Animal welfare first: seek prompt help from a qualified veterinary professional for serious or worsening illness. Difficulty breathing, collapse, seizures, severe bleeding, suspected poisoning, difficult birth or several animals suddenly becoming ill require urgent veterinary attention. Do not delay care to use an AI tool.
